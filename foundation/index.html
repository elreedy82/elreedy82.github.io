Motion and reduced motion

Motion is restrained. With “reduce motion” enabled in the operating system the animation stops and the dot sits at its final position.

The reduced-motion final state is also what a visitor with motion disabled sees immediately.

Block rendering

The remaining blocks from content/pages/foundation.yaml render through the same registry. A block type without a component fails the build instead of rendering nothing.

What this milestone proves

  • Content is typed in content/pages/foundation.yaml and rendered through one block registry.
  • Day and Night share one semantic token set; no component hard-codes a colour.
  • Body text stays at 18 px or larger at every breakpoint.
  • The header, footer and page shell come from content/site.yaml.
