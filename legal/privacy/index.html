Website privacy policy

Version 1.0 — 10 November 2026

This policy describes this website, reedoapps.com. The Watchkeeper app has its own rules inside the licence agreement: see Terms 1.2, sections 8 and 9.

Who we are

This website is operated by Mohamed Adel Elreedy, Dubai, United Arab Emirates, owner and developer of ReEdO Watchkeeper. Contact for anything in this policy: support@reedoapps.com.

What this website collects

  • No account and no cookies by default. The website does not set cookies, does not use advertising or tracking scripts, and does not build a profile of you.
  • Request form. If you send a request, the details you type are used to answer it: name, company, e-mail address, the level you are interested in, the number of users and your message. The form opens your own e-mail application; you can review the message before it is sent.
  • Server logs. The hosting provider may record technical information such as your IP address and browser type for security and to keep the site working. These logs are not used to identify visitors.
  • Analytics is off. The website has no analytics enabled. If Cloudflare Web Analytics is switched on later, it counts visits without cookies and without identifying people; this page will say so.

How the information is used

Request details are used only to answer your request, prepare a quote, and keep the licensing records needed to support you. They are not sold, and they are not used for marketing without your agreement.

Where it is processed

The website is served from IONOS webspace, with DNS and delivery through Cloudflare. The ReEdO licensing server runs on Cloudflare and may process data outside your country. E-mail is handled by the e-mail provider used for support@reedoapps.com.

How long we keep it

Request details are kept for as long as needed to answer the request and support the licence. Licence records are kept for the period in Terms 1.2, section 9.3 (as long as the licence runs and up to three years after it ends, or longer where the law requires).

Your choices

You may ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of the personal data held about you and your requests at support@reedoapps.com. Deleting licence data ends the licence. You may also complain to the data-protection authority that applies to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and date are published on this page. Material changes are described in plain words rather than hidden in a new document.
