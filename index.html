Questions

Good to know.

Does my audio leave my PC?

No. Speech becomes text on your PC. Only the text needed for an answer or the minutes goes to ReEdO AI, and it keeps none of it.

Does it work with Teams, Zoom and Meet?

Yes. Watchkeeper listens to the sound coming from your PC, so any meeting program works — and so do meetings in a room.

Which languages?

English and 24 European languages out of the box. Arabic and 99 more languages come with the optional Whisper model.

Do I need my own AI account?

No. ReEdO AI is included in every plan. Pro and Premium can add their own accounts too.

What if I am offline?

Transcription and recording keep working. AI answers return when the connection does. The licence also works offline for 14 days.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Install it and request a trial from the app. Higher levels can be tried for up to 3 days.

Does the AI change what people said?

No. Watchkeeper keeps every version of a line it heard. If two listenings differ, it merges them into one line using only words that were heard — or names from your meeting. Hover a line to see what was heard.

Can it carry open actions from one meeting to the next?

Yes, with Projects (Premium). Each open point keeps its number and history until it's closed, and today's meeting starts with the open points on the agenda.

Are the minutes written before I check the names?

No. After the meeting you review the mis-heard words first — and the points in a project meeting. Only then are the summary and minutes written, with your corrections.
