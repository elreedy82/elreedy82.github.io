Changelog

What's new.

Every version, in plain words. Customer-facing changes only — nothing about servers or internal tools.

Updates arrive inside the app: Settings → About → Updates shows what is new and installs a signed version only when you choose. A meeting is never interrupted by an update.

Versions

Release

Version 1.3.0

November 2026

Points closed since the last meeting, reasons for On hold and Cancelled, real due dates, the project register export and the professional writing style.

  • Points closed since the last meeting. Done and Cancelled print separately: as a full table, one line, or hidden. Done is hidden by default; Cancelled prints with its reason. Open, In progress and On hold always print. Hiding changes only the document.
  • Reasons and real due dates. On hold and Cancelled need a reason, asked wherever a status is set. A point can carry an optional real due date next to the free-text due; event-based dues show as Milestone. On hold is never marked overdue.
  • Project register. Export the register to Excel (summary, one sheet per status, history of every status change) or PDF (summary page, one section per status), with the minutes logo, the statuses, one owner and an as-of date. Overdue is red and due within three days is amber where a real date exists.
  • Professional writing style. The meeting brief and agenda, Live summary, Questions and Points use short factual lines, no first person, with a header line, Objective and Focus, fixed status tags and numbered information requests and conflicts to resolve.

Release

Version 1.2.0

November 2026

Projects and the Points log, Start today's meeting, the Points tab, Review points, the points table in the minutes, moving meetings, project reports and Backup and Restore.

  • Projects. Group the meetings of one job - a drydock, a repowering, a weekly committee - and keep every open point numbered, owned and dated across meetings.
  • Start today's meeting. The new meeting opens with the open points as agenda item 1, the regular participants and a brief of what changed since last time.
  • Points tab and Review points. Open points light up as they are discussed; after the meeting one pass suggests status changes, notes and new points, each with the sentence it came from. Nothing changes until you confirm.
  • Minutes with the points table. One table carries every open point forward; points closed since the last meeting print once.
  • Move meetings. Move a meeting into, out of or between projects; nothing is deleted and Undo brings it back.
  • Project register and reports. Export the register to Excel or PDF, and weekly or monthly progress reports in Word or PDF.
  • Backup and Restore. Copy meetings, projects and the People book to OneDrive, Google Drive or a USB disk, and restore them on a new PC.

Release

Version 1.1.0

10 November 2026

Review reminders, licence codes locked to one PC, instant licence changes and Talk as an add-on.

  • Corrections you have not reviewed yet. The Meeting tab shows how many probably mis-heard words are waiting, and reminds you before the minutes or the Word/PDF export. Nothing changes until you accept.
  • Settings > Meetings shows the words waiting next to each meeting; a click opens it.
  • Licence codes can be locked to one PC. An unused code stops working after two days, and support can renew it or issue a new one.
  • Licence changes reach the PC at once: a level change, an add-on or a released PC is picked up immediately, and the app checks in every two hours.
  • Talk is an add-on for any level: request it from the app. Without it, Talk shows a badge and a Request Talk button.
  • Plan text and the website address can be updated from the licence system without an app update, and appear on the upgrade card and About page.
  • Updates show what is new inside the app and install only signed versions, never while a meeting records.
  • Smaller fixes: the Eyes follow the mouse anywhere again, and the no-internet message explains that transcription continues.

Release

Version 1.0.0 — online edition

November 2026

Three licence levels, an online licence that works offline for 14 days, trials and ReEdO AI included.

  • Three levels: Standard (online meetings, live transcript, answers, summaries, minutes), Pro (adds Camera view, Auto answers, meeting brief, speaker names and the People book) and Premium (adds in-the-room and hybrid meetings, Ask my meetings and open actions, minutes styles and Excel import).
  • Your data is never locked: after a downgrade or a trial, every meeting, summary, minutes file and People book entry still opens and exports.
  • Online licence: enter a code once (WK-...). Watchkeeper checks the licence once a day, keeps working offline for 14 days, and picks up upgrades, renewals or a released PC at the next check.
  • Trials: try the next level for one to three days. The trial code must be entered within 24 hours and the trial starts when it is entered.
  • ReEdO AI included: no AI account needed. Each month includes 15, 30 or 60 AI meeting-hours; when the allowance is used up, AI pauses but transcription and recording keep working.
  • Requests from the app: a new licence, renewal, upgrade or trial request reaches support, with e-mail as the fallback. Contact: support@reedoapps.com.
  • Licence terms 1.1 accepted at first start.
