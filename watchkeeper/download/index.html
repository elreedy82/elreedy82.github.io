Requirements

What your PC needs.

A normal Windows laptop or desktop from the last few years is enough.

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)

    A current, supported Windows installation.

  • 8 GB RAM recommended

    For speech recognition and the meeting window to run together.

  • 2 GB free disk for speech models

    The models download once.

  • A microphone

    A headset, the laptop microphone, or a room microphone.

  • Internet for activation and ReEdO AI

    Transcription and recording keep working offline. The licence also works offline for 14 days.

Install

Four steps, once.

The same steps in the app and in the video tour (V02).

  1. Run the installer

    The installer is signed by ReEdO; Windows may ask you to confirm.

  2. Accept the licence terms

    Terms 1.2 appear at first start and are accepted once.

  3. Download the speech models

    About 700 MB, once. After that, speech is written on your PC.

  4. Enter your licence or request a trial

    A licence code, a trial code from the app, or a request to support@reedoapps.com.

Already installed?

Watchkeeper updates itself: open Settings → About → Updates to see what is new and install the signed update when you choose. A meeting is never interrupted by an update.
