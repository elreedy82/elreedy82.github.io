Trial

Try the next level first.

You can try a higher level before you commit. Enter the trial code within 24 hours of issue; the trial starts when you enter it.

  1. Install Watchkeeper

    Install it, accept the terms, and request a licence code or a trial in the app.

  2. Request a trial

    Ask for the next level in the app. We reply by e-mail with a trial code.

  3. Enter the code

    Enter the code within 24 hours. The trial starts the moment you do.

  4. 1 to 3 days

    Use the higher level for one to three days. When the trial ends, the app returns to your licence level.

Buying questions

Before you ask for a licence.

Can I pay on the website?

Not yet. There's no online payment at launch: send a request, we reply with a quote and payment details by e-mail, and the licence arrives after payment.

How much does it cost?

We show the price from the licence system once that connection is live. Until then, the plans ask us for the price — send a request and we'll reply with a quote.

Does my licence renew automatically?

No. A licence doesn't renew by itself. Watchkeeper reminds you before the period ends, and renewal happens when you ask for it and pay.

Can I move Watchkeeper to a new PC?

Yes, with our help: ask support to release the licence from the old PC. A licence code normally works on one computer.

Can I get an invoice or a company licence?

Ask support@reedoapps.com. We'll tell you by e-mail what can be issued for your case rather than promise it on the website.

What is the refund policy?

Terms 1.2: if the Software cannot be activated or does not run on your computer and support cannot solve it, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of buying the licence. Otherwise fees are not refundable, except where the terms say so or the law requires it.

Can I try a higher level?

Yes, at the Owner's discretion: Standard can try Pro, and Pro can try Premium, for one to three days. Enter the trial code within 24 hours; the trial starts when you enter it.
