Features

Everything you need in one meeting.

Everything the meeting needs, from the agenda to the minutes.

  • Live transcript

    On your PC, as people speak.

    • Every line is written on your own computer; nothing is uploaded to become a transcript.
    • Long talk is cut at a quiet moment, so a sentence is not broken mid-word.

  • Live answers

    The answer appears in seconds.

    • Answers use the meeting brief, the documents and what has been said so far.

  • Auto / Suggest

    Answer automatically, or open a suggestion.

    • Auto shows the answer straight away; Suggest keeps it closed until you open it.

  • Camera view

    Pro

    The answer sits under your webcam.

    • The answer window stays out of a shared screen or recording. Pro or higher.

  • Live summary

    Decisions and actions as they happen.

    • Decisions and actions are collected while the meeting runs, so you can follow the direction without scrolling back.

  • Questions to ask

    Prepared from the brief and agenda.

    • Prepared from the brief, the agenda, the documents and your role in the meeting.

  • Meeting brief

    Documents become one page.

    • PDF, Word and Excel documents are read before the meeting and turned into one page.

  • Speaker names

    People book and labels.

    • Speakers are labelled in the transcript, and a label can be corrected to a person in the People book.

  • In the room

    One microphone, hybrid too.

    • One microphone covers the room, and your own lines are recognised by your voice. Premium.

  • Minutes

    Word and PDF, your logo.

    • Decisions and actions with owners and dates, exported to Word or PDF with your company logo.

  • Ask my meetings

    What was agreed, and where.

    • Ask past meetings a question and follow the references back to the lines that support the answer.

  • Talk

    Add-on

    Dictate anywhere with Z+X.

    • Talk types what you say into Outlook, Teams, WhatsApp, Word, Excel or a browser. An add-on for any level.

  • Projects and Points log

    Premium

    Open points carried from meeting to meeting until closed.

    • Every point keeps its number, owner, due date, status and history until it is closed. Premium.

  • Review first, then write

    Your corrections reach the summary and the minutes.

    • After Stop you review mis-heard words (and the points in a project meeting) before the summary and minutes are written.

Questions

Good to know.

Does my audio leave my PC?

No. Speech becomes text on your PC. Only the text needed for an answer or the minutes goes to ReEdO AI, and it keeps none of it.

Does it work with Teams, Zoom and Meet?

Yes. Watchkeeper listens to the sound coming from your PC, so any meeting program works — and so do meetings in a room.

Which languages?

English and 24 European languages out of the box. Arabic and 99 more languages come with the optional Whisper model.

Do I need my own AI account?

No. ReEdO AI is included in every plan. Pro and Premium can add their own accounts too.

What if I am offline?

Transcription and recording keep working. AI answers return when the connection does. The licence also works offline for 14 days.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Install it and request a trial from the app. Higher levels can be tried for up to 3 days.

Does the AI change what people said?

No. Watchkeeper keeps every version of a line it heard. If two listenings differ, it merges them into one line using only words that were heard — or names from your meeting. Hover a line to see what was heard.

Can it carry open actions from one meeting to the next?

Yes, with Projects (Premium). Each open point keeps its number and history until it's closed, and today's meeting starts with the open points on the agenda.

Are the minutes written before I check the names?

No. After the meeting you review the mis-heard words first — and the points in a project meeting. Only then are the summary and minutes written, with your corrections.
