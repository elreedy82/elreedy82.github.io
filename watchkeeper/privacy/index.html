Privacy and security

Private by design, honest by default.

Speech becomes text on your PC, and your meeting data stays there. Only the text needed for an answer, summary, minutes or Talk is sent — and the ReEdO server doesn't keep it.

The path of a question

What leaves your PC, and what does not.

Your audio, recording, transcript, meetings and minutes stay on your computer. Only the few lines needed for an answer travel, over an encrypted connection; ReEdO AI keeps none of that text.

The facts

What is kept, and where.

What stays on your PC
Speech, transcripts, summaries, minutes, documents, recordings and voice prints. Meeting data is stored on your computer only.
What is sent
Only the text needed for an answer, summary, minutes, meeting brief or Talk. It travels over an encrypted connection to the ReEdO server, which doesn't store it.
What the licence keeps
The licence id, the Machine Code (made from your Windows installation, with no personal data or files) and the app version. The server records the licence, time, model, tokens and cost to count the allowance.
No app telemetry
The software sends no usage data or telemetry. A licence check sends only the licence id, Machine Code and version.
How updates are protected
Updates install only when signed by ReEdO. The signature also carries the version, so an old installer can't be passed off as new.
Where AI runs
ReEdO AI comes with the licence and runs through api.reedoapps.com. Pro and Premium can use their own AI accounts instead.

Providers

Which AI services may see the text.

The ReEdO server picks a provider that doesn't keep or train on the text where that choice is offered, and may switch to a backup if a provider fails.

ReEdO AI (included)
Routed through the ReEdO server to a provider chosen by the Owner. The server passes the needed text and returns the answer; it keeps none of it.
Provider preference
Providers that don't keep or train on the text are chosen first (for example through OpenRouter).
Backup providers
If a provider fails, the server may switch to a backup. Backups can include services hosted outside your country, including China (for example DeepSeek, GLM or MiniMax).
Your own AI account
With Pro and Premium you can use your own AI account instead. That text is handled under that provider's own terms and privacy policy.

Licence data and your rights

The Owner keeps licence data (name, company, e-mail, licence details, Machine Codes, check times, versions, AI use and cost, requests) to provide, protect and support the licence — for as long as it runs and up to three years after it ends, or longer where the law requires. It is not sold, and it is held on Cloudflare servers that may be outside your country.

You may ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of your licence data at support@reedoapps.com. Deletion ends the licence.

The full legal text, including the recording-consent, AI-output and liability sections, is in Terms 1.2. The website's own privacy policy is at privacy policy.
