Before the meeting

Before

Meeting workspace

Every meeting gets its own workspace: the brief, the agenda, the documents and the people, kept together.

Before

Brief and agenda

Keep the short brief and the agenda in the meeting, so the assistant knows what the meeting is for.

Before

Documents

Add PDF, Word and Excel files. Watchkeeper reads them so the answers and the minutes use the right context.

Before

Meeting brief from documents

The assistant turns the agenda and documents into one page: what is being decided, what to watch for, and who is involved.

Before

Questions to ask

A list of questions worth asking, prepared from the brief, the agenda and your role in the meeting.

Before

Participants and People book

Keep the people you meet with, with their company and role, so names are spelled the same way in every meeting.

During the meeting

During

Live transcript on your PC

Speech is written to text as people speak, on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded to become a transcript. Long talk is no longer cut mid-word, and short hesitations no longer split a sentence.

During

Nothing lost on screen

The grey live text and the second listening are both kept. If the second listening loses words, the live text stays. A line that appeared never disappears.

During

One clean line

About every minute, lines heard differently are merged into one in the background - only with words that were heard, or names from your meeting. Hover to see every version.

During

Premium

Points tab

Open points light up as they are discussed, with the sentence they came from and a suggested status. New points are never added automatically during the meeting - you can add one by hand at any time.

During

Question detection

A question put to you is spotted in the conversation and marked, so you never lose the thread.

During

Live answers — Auto and Suggest

Choose Auto to show the answer straight away, or Suggest to open a short suggestion first. Either way the answer uses the meeting context.

During

Pro

Camera view

The answer appears in a small window under your webcam, so you keep eye contact and it stays out of a shared screen or recording.

During

Live summary

Decisions and actions are summarised while the meeting runs, so you can check the direction without scrolling back.

During

Questions tab

Questions worth asking are kept in one tab, in the order you may want to raise them.

During

Speaker names

Speakers are labelled in the transcript, and you can correct a name to a person in the People book.

During

In the room and hybrid

One microphone in a room, or a room plus people online, works in the same meeting window.

During

Echo cancellation

With laptop speakers, the meeting sound is taken out of the microphone signal so your own transcript stays clean.

During

Remote Desktop

Over Remote Desktop, Watchkeeper listens to the microphone of the device you connect from; the audio settings explain what is happening.

After the meeting

After

Clean-up listen

After Stop, every line is listened to again from the recording, on your PC, and merged with what was heard live.

After

Review first, then write

1. Mis-heard words and names. 2. Points (project meetings). 3. Only then the final summary and minutes, with your corrections. Nothing to review? Writing starts at once. In a hurry? Write now.

After

Review mis-heard words

Watchkeeper lists the words that were probably mis-heard, with the reason and where they are. Nothing changes until you accept. A correction you accept also fixes the AI's own spelling of the name in the summary and minutes.

After

Participants filled in for you

People who spoke and were named are added to the participants and to the minutes' attendees. Names from your People book mentioned in the meeting are suggested. Rename a speaker and the minutes follow.

After

Minutes in Word and PDF

Professional third-person minutes with your style and logo, exported in Word or PDF. Every topic, decision, action and point starts with a short subject in bold. The brief, agenda, summaries and points use a professional writing style - no first person, short factual lines.

After

Backup and Restore

Copy meetings, projects and the People book to your OneDrive, Google Drive or a USB disk, and bring them back on a new PC.

Open the Backup and Restore guide

After

Final summary

Press Stop and the final summary is written: decisions, actions and the points that matter.

After

MP3 recording

Keep the meeting recording as an MP3, on your PC, alongside the transcript.

After

Ask my meetings

Ask any past meeting what was agreed, with references back to the lines that support the answer.

After

Open actions

Actions with owners and dates are listed after the meeting, so nothing agreed is left without a name.

Projects

Projects

Premium

Projects

Group the meetings of one job. Create a project from the Projects screen, the New meeting window or Move this meeting.

Projects

Points log

Every point numbered (MVX-001), with owner, due date, status (Open, In progress, On hold, Done, Cancelled) and its history meeting by meeting. On hold and Cancelled need a reason. Overdue is shown automatically.

Projects

Start today's meeting

The new meeting opens with the open points as agenda item 1, the regular participants, and a brief of what changed since last time.

Projects

Review points

After the meeting one reasoning pass reads the whole meeting and suggests status changes, notes and new points, each with the sentence it came from. Nothing changes until you confirm.

Projects

Minutes with the points table

One table carries every open point forward; points closed since the last meeting print once.

Projects

Project register and reports

Export the register to Excel (summary, one sheet per status, history; subject in its own column) or PDF; weekly and monthly progress reports in Word or PDF.

Projects

Move meetings

Move a meeting into, out of or between projects; nothing is deleted and Undo brings it back.

Projects stay readable and exportable on every level; running project meetings and the AI suggestions need Premium (3-day trial from Pro).

Talk

Talk

Add-on

Push to talk

Hold Z and X, speak, let go: the words are typed where the cursor is — Outlook, Teams, WhatsApp, Word, a browser.

Talk

Notes

Hold Z and C to save a note instead of typing it, so a thought is kept without interrupting the text box.

Talk

Clean-up

Filler words, repeated words and false starts are removed, and a wrong figure or name is caught before the text is pasted.

Talk

History

Dictations and notes are kept for a few days, encrypted with your Windows account, and can be searched, pinned or deleted.

Talk

The Eyes

A small face on top of every program looks at the cursor and widens when you speak; a click starts or stops dictation.

Talk

Works over Remote Desktop

On an iPad, tablet or another PC, tap Z then X to start and stop; characters sent by touch keyboards are understood.

Privacy and licences

Privacy

On-PC speech

Speech is written to text on your computer. Meeting data is stored on your computer only.

Privacy

No telemetry

The app sends no usage data. A licence check sends only the licence id, the Machine Code and the version.

Privacy

ReEdO AI keeps nothing

Only the text needed for an answer is sent, and the ReEdO server does not store it.

Privacy

Signed updates

A new version installs only after you choose to install it, and only when it is signed by ReEdO.
