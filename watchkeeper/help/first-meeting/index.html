Help

Your first meeting

From New meeting to the minutes, step by step.

One. Create the meeting

Choose File > New meeting (Ctrl+N) and give it a name. The name is required. Watchkeeper makes a folder for the meeting on your PC.

Two. Prepare (optional)

Open the Meeting tab. You can set your role (Evaluating, Presenting or Following up), a brief, an agenda, the participants and documents (PDF, Word, Excel). With Pro or higher, Watchkeeper prepares a meeting brief from your documents before the meeting.

Three. Start

Pick your situation and press Start. Speech is written on your PC. Questions put to you get an answer automatically (Auto) or with an Answer? button (Suggest). Off turns this off.

Four. During the meeting

  • Ctrl+Space answers the last thing said.
  • Type a question in the question box and press Ctrl+Enter, or leave it empty and press Answer to reply to the last thing said.
  • Select lines in the transcript and press Ask about selected lines.
  • Hover over a THEM line and press Answer this.
  • Click Me to mute your microphone.
  • Live summary is updated every few minutes.

Five. Stop

Press Stop. Watchkeeper converts the recording to MP3, writes the final summary, drafts the minutes (if Draft automatically is on) and checks the transcript for words that were probably heard wrongly.

Six. After the meeting

  • Open the Meeting tab and review the words that were probably heard wrongly. Nothing changes until you accept it.
  • Read the final summary and the minutes.
  • Export the summary or the minutes as Word or PDF from the File menu or the Minutes tab.

Fixing a wrong word

Select the word in the transcript, right-click and choose Correct. You can apply the correction to this line, to this meeting, or always. The Corrections table in Settings > Speech keeps your always-corrections. Premium can export and import it as Excel.

Shortcuts

Scroll horizontally to see all columns.

Shortcut Action
Ctrl+N New meeting
Ctrl+O Open meeting
Ctrl+Space Answer the last thing said
Ctrl+Enter Send the question box
Ctrl+Alt+H Show or hide Watchkeeper

More shortcuts are in the File menu and in the articles on Camera view and Talk.

Closing the window

The X button hides Watchkeeper to the tray (it asks once). The tray icon shows a red dot while it records. To leave completely, use Quit in the tray menu. If it is recording, it asks whether to stop and save first.
