After the meeting: review first

What happens after Stop, in what order, and why the minutes wait for your review.

Press Stop and Watchkeeper works through the meeting in a fixed order. A status line shows the stage.

The order after Stop

  1. Clean-up listen. Every line is listened to again from the recording, on your PC, and merged with what was heard live. Lines where the other side spoke at the same time are skipped.
  2. Review 1: mis-heard words, names and participants. You review the words and names that were probably heard wrongly, and the participants filled in from the meeting.
  3. Review 2: points. In a project meeting, you confirm the suggested status changes, notes and new points.
  4. Final summary and minutes. Only then are they written, with your corrections.

Nothing to review? Writing starts at once. In a hurry, Write now skips the rest of the review.

Why the minutes wait

The summary and minutes are written after your review, so the spelling and wording you accept are the ones used in the documents. A correction you accept also fixes the AI's own spelling of the name in the summary and minutes.

What a line shows

Hover over a transcript line to see every version that was heard. Both listenings are kept: the grey live text and the second listening. If the second listening loses words or comes back empty, the live text stays - a line that appeared on screen never disappears. About every minute, lines heard differently are merged into one using only words that were heard, or names from your meeting.
