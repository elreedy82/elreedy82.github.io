Licence, levels and trials

Licence code, Standard / Pro / Premium, trials, offline use and renewal.

Your licence code

A licence code looks like WK-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. You enter it once.

  1. Open Settings > About and press Enter a code or key, or enter it on the first screen.
  2. Press Apply. Your PC needs the internet for this step.

Each code works on one PC. It can be locked to the PC you requested it from, so it will not work on another PC. Use your code within 2 days of receiving it.

Your Machine Code

The Machine Code identifies your PC. It is in Settings > About, with a Copy button. When you request a licence in the app, it is sent with your request.

Levels

There are three levels: Standard, Pro and Premium. See the comparison on the Pricing page. The level shows in the top bar. Features of a higher level show a Pro or Premium badge, and a click opens the upgrade card.

Your data is never locked. If your licence ends or you move to a lower level, every meeting, summary, minutes file and People book entry still opens and exports.

ReEdO AI hours

ReEdO AI is included. The monthly allowance is 15 hours for Standard, 30 for Pro and 60 for Premium. Settings > About shows how much you have used this month.

Trial

You can try the next level up for 1 to 3 days.

  1. Press Try it for up to 3 days on a locked feature or in Settings > About.
  2. Send the request. We send a trial code by e-mail. It looks like TR-XXXX-XXXX.
  3. Enter it within 24 hours in Settings > About > Enter a code or key. The trial starts when you enter it.

The top bar shows the time left, with a reminder on the last day. When the trial ends, Watchkeeper returns to your licence level by itself.

Working offline

After a successful check, your licence keeps working for 14 days without internet. After that, connect once. The app checks at each start and every 2 hours when it is online. You can also press Check now in Settings > About.

Upgrade, renewal and Talk

Press Request upgrade, Request renewal or Request Talk in Settings > About. If sending fails, the app offers to send the request by e-mail to support@reedoapps.com. A change to your level reaches your PC within minutes. If it does not show, press Check now.

If your licence ends

The activation screen shows the reason. Your meetings and exports stay available.
