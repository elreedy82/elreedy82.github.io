Help

Talk: dictate anywhere

Speak instead of typing, in any program. Talk is an add-on.

Talk types what you say wherever your cursor is: Outlook, Teams, WhatsApp, Word, Excel or a browser.

Dictate

  1. Click where you want the text.
  2. Hold Z + X and speak.
  3. Let go. Your words are typed.

To make a note instead, hold Z + C. A note is saved but not typed. Press Esc to cancel. Over Remote Desktop or with a touch keyboard, tap Z then X to start and again to stop.

What happens to your words

Speech is written on your PC and corrected with your Corrections table. Talk then cleans it: fillers, repeats and false starts. The AI clean-up is checked. If it changes a figure or takes too long, your PC's own clean-up is pasted instead. Talk never types into a password box.

The Eyes

The Eyes are a small face that stays on top of your programs. The eyes follow the mouse and the mouth moves with your voice. Hover over it for Dictate, Note, History and Settings. It is hidden from screen sharing and in full-screen programs, and you can drag it anywhere.

Talk history

Every dictation and note is kept for 7 days (pinned ones are kept). The history is encrypted with your Windows account.

During a meeting

Talk is paused while a meeting records. You can allow it in Settings > Talk.

Getting Talk

Talk is an add-on that you can add to any level. If it is not part of your licence, the Talk settings are dimmed. Press Request Talk in Settings > Talk.
