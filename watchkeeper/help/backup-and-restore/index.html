Backup and Restore

Copy your meetings to OneDrive, Google Drive or a USB disk, and bring them back.

Backup is available at every level. Watchkeeper copies your meetings into a folder you choose. If that folder is kept in sync by Google Drive for desktop or OneDrive, their program uploads the copies. Watchkeeper never signs in to Google or Microsoft. A USB disk or network drive works too.

Set it up

Open Settings > Backup.

  • Backup folder: press Choose, or click a folder found on your PC (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox).
  • Back up now: copies only what changed since the last backup. The meeting being recorded is skipped and copied next time. Meetings you delete on your PC stay in the backup, marked deleted.
  • After each meeting: a backup runs about 3 minutes after you press Stop.
  • Include the recordings: on by default. Switch it off to back up only transcripts, minutes and documents.
  • Reminder: a note appears in the Meetings list when the last backup is older than 1, 3, 7 (default), 14 or 30 days.

Your licence and AI keys are never included in a backup.

Backup password

You can protect the backup with a password. Every file is encrypted before it leaves Watchkeeper. Without a password, the backup is made of ordinary zip files that Windows can open.

If you lose the password, the backup cannot be opened by anyone, including us. Keep it somewhere safe.

Company meetings

Recordings and minutes of company meetings are company information. For work meetings, the company OneDrive is usually the right place.

Restore

  1. Settings > Backup > Restore...
  2. Choose the folder that holds ReEdO Watchkeeper Backup.
  3. Tick the meetings, projects, or Settings, People book and logo you want.
  4. Enter the password if the backup has one, then press Restore.

Nothing on your PC is overwritten. A meeting that exists already comes back beside it as "name (restored)". An existing project keeps its Points log, and the backup's log is saved next to it as a separate file. Restored settings are used at once, and the previous ones are kept in a before-restore folder.
