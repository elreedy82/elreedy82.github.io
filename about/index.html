About

Built for the meetings I sit in.

ReEdO Watchkeeper is an independent product from Dubai, built by a marine chief engineer for real technical meetings — yards, class surveyors, vendors, and the people who run them.

The story

Why Watchkeeper exists.

I'm a marine chief engineer. Most of my working week is technical meetings: drydock planning, class surveys, vendor clarifications, repair specifications. The talk is fast and specific — equipment tags, dates, responsibilities — and the minutes matter as much as the meeting.

I wanted three things that didn't exist together: answers to the questions put to me while the meeting is still running, minutes that read like a professional record, and the certainty that my voice and my meetings stay on my own PC.

So I built Watchkeeper. It listens on the PC, writes the transcript there, answers when someone puts a question to you, and turns the summary and minutes into Word or PDF. Only the text needed for an answer leaves the machine — and ReEdO AI keeps none of it.

The first version arrived in October 2026. The online edition — licence checks, ReEdO AI in every level, and the plans on this website — arrived in November 2026 from Dubai, where the product is made.

What we hold to

Four values, no slogans.

  • 01

    Right

    A meeting record has to be accurate. Figures, dates, names and decisions stay exact, and mis-heard words are fixed before the minutes go out.

  • 02

    Fast

    An answer that lands after the conversation has moved on isn't an answer. Live answers are built for seconds, not minutes.

  • 03

    Private

    Speech becomes text on your PC, and your meeting data stays there. Only the text needed for an answer is sent; ReEdO AI keeps none of it.

  • 04

    Practical

    Built for the way meetings really run: online, in a room, hybrid, over Remote Desktop, with the tools people already use.
